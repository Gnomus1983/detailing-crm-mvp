# COMPETITIVE INSIGHTS

## EasyWeek

Strong sides:

- self-service onboarding
- online booking
- wide horizontal coverage across many niches
- clean acquisition flow for quick signup

What we take:

- simplicity of booking entry
- future storefront / widget logic
- low-friction first step for small businesses

What we do not copy:

- generic “for everyone” positioning
- horizontal product identity
- product structure or copy

## Carbook

Strong sides:

- vertical auto-service framing
- live sales process
- guided onboarding
- training and trust-building
- deeper STO logic and heavier operational depth

What we take:

- human onboarding as an advantage
- vertical confidence for auto services
- implementation and training as part of the offer

What we do not copy:

- branding
- design
- texts
- enterprise STO-heavy product depth for the current MVP

## Our Strategy

We are building:

- local vertical CRM for auto services in Moldova
- lighter and simpler than heavy STO systems
- more guided and local than cold global self-service tools

Core product edge:

- Telegram-first operating loop
- before / after photos
- customer status page by link
- Russian / Romanian context
- guided onboarding and launch help

## What This Means For Product Direction

Near-term:

- stronger storefront positioning for detailing / wash / tire / auto studio
- richer work-order statuses
- stronger customer status experience
- clearer owner / manager / master role views

Later:

- no-show protection
- embedded booking widget
- white-label / branded app as a premium layer
